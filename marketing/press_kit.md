## Short description

**EN:** You only need one stamp. A free daily browser game about getting one document stamped at a Brazilian notary office before the windows close for lunch. Today's bureaucracy is generated, and it's the same for everyone.

**PT-BR:** Você só precisa de um carimbo. Todo dia, uma burocracia nova, igual pra todo mundo. Descubra a ordem dos guichês, preencha o que mandarem e saia antes do almoço.

## Highlights

- A new chain of forms, windows and queues every day, the same for every player.
- Five windows with fixed manias: blue pen only, a copier that jams on every fourth copy, a lunch break that moves every day.
- Every form has one trap for people who fill in on autopilot.
- Paste your result: time, forms, send-backs and your path through the windows.
- Every chain is generated with a guarantee: it can be finished, and in time.
